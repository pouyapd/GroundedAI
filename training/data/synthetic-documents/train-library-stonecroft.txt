Stonecroft Library - Lending Rules

1. About Stonecroft Library

Stonecroft Library serves the local area. Members can suggest new books at the desk.

2. The loan period for books

Books may be borrowed for 35 days. Printing costs 17 cents per page. Please handle rare books with care.

3. How many times an item can be renewed

An item can be renewed three times. A replacement card costs 4 euros. Members can suggest new books at the desk.

4. The weekday opening time

On weekdays the library opens at 10:30. Members may borrow up to 10 items at a time. Members can suggest new books at the desk.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Stonecroft Library help desk.
