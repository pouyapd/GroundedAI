Gullstone Harbour Marina – Berth Holder Regulations

Gullstone Harbour Marina is owned and run by the Gullstone Harbour Trust. The marina has 420 berths on eight pontoons, labelled A to H. These regulations apply to everyone who holds an annual berth licence, and to their crew and guests. The Marina Manager is Siobhan Achterberg, and the Harbour Master is Captain Rafael Okoro-Lunn, who is responsible for safety and navigation within the harbour limits.

An annual berth licence runs from 1 April to 31 March. Licence fees are charged per metre of the vessel's overall length, including bowsprits, davits and outboard brackets. The fee for the 2026–27 licence year is €395 per metre on pontoons A to E and €350 per metre on pontoons F to H, including VAT. The minimum charge is for a length of 8 metres, so a 6.5-metre boat pays the same as an 8-metre boat.

The full fee is due by 1 April. Berth holders may instead pay by 10 monthly direct debits from April to January, with a surcharge of 4% on the total fee. Existing berth holders must confirm that they want to renew their licence by 15 February. Berths that have not been renewed by that date may be offered to the next person on the waiting list.

Anyone may join the waiting list by paying a deposit of €250. The deposit is taken off the first licence fee when a berth is offered, or returned in full if the person leaves the list. Berths are offered in order of the date of joining, but a berth can only be offered if it suits the length, beam and draught of the vessel.

Every vessel must have third-party liability insurance of at least €3,000,000. Berth holders must send a copy of their insurance certificate to the marina office each year before 1 April, and a vessel without valid insurance may be refused entry or moved. The vessel must also be kept in a seaworthy condition and must clearly show its name.

The speed limit is 3 knots inside the marina and 5 knots in the approach channel. Skippers should call the marina office on VHF channel 80 before arrival or departure. The marina office is open 08:00–20:00 from May to September and 09:00–17:00 from October to April.

Shore power is metered at each berth and charged at €0.38 per kWh. The maximum supply is 16 amps per berth. Shore power cables must be disconnected whenever a vessel is left unattended for more than 7 days, unless a heater or dehumidifier has been approved by the office.

Berth holders may sleep on board for up to 3 nights in any week without permission. Living on board for longer requires a liveaboard permit, which costs €600 per year, and no more than 15 liveaboard permits are issued at any time. Sub-letting a berth is not allowed. Berths may only be swapped through the marina office.

Berth holders should tell the office when their vessel will be away from its berth for more than 48 hours, so that the berth can be used for visitors. Berth holders who report an absence of 7 nights or more receive a credit of 20% of the visitor fees earned on their berth, up to €300 per licence year. The credit is applied to the next licence invoice.

The fuel pontoon is open 08:30–18:00 every day from April to October. Engines must be switched off while fuelling, and smoking is not allowed anywhere on the fuel pontoon. Oily waste and used engine oil must be taken to the collection point next to the boatyard. The black water pump-out at the end of pontoon D is free for berth holders.

Hot work, such as welding, grinding or the use of open flames, is not allowed on the pontoons without a Hot Work Permit (form HW-3) from the Harbour Master, which must be requested at least 24 hours in advance. Antifouling and hull scraping may only be done in the boatyard. A lift-out costs €14 per metre, and storage on the hard costs €6.50 per metre per week.

Each berth licence includes one permit for the berth holders' car park, and further permits cost €45 per year. Berth holders may bring up to 6 guests onto the pontoons at any one time, and guests must be accompanied by the berth holder or a named crew member. Children under 12 must wear a lifejacket on the pontoons.

Between 1 November and 28 February, berth holders must check their mooring lines and fenders at least once every 14 days, or name a keyholder who will do this for them. The marina offers a boat-check service for €35 per month, which includes a weekly visual check and a written report after any storm warning.

Dogs are welcome but must be kept on a lead on the pontoons and in the car park. The code for the pontoon gates changes on the first day of each month and is sent to berth holders by text message.

The Trust may end a licence with 28 days' written notice if the berth holder seriously or repeatedly breaks these regulations. A berth holder may end the licence by giving 3 months' written notice. In that case the unused part of the fee is refunded pro rata, minus an administration charge of €150.
