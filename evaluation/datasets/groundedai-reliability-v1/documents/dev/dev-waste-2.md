Thornmere Waste Services – Frequently Asked Questions and Christmas Collections

How do I find my collection day? Enter your postcode in the collection day checker on the Thornmere District Council website. The district is divided into 10 collection rounds, named Round A to Round J, and each round has a fixed weekday. Your grey and blue bins are emptied on alternate weeks, and your brown food caddy is emptied every week on the same day.

What time should my bins be out? Put your containers at the kerbside by 06:30 on your collection day. Crews work until 16:00, so please do not report a bin as missed before 16:30. Bring your bins back in by 20:00 the same day.

My bin was missed. What should I do? Report it online or by phone within two working days of your collection day. You will receive a reference number starting with MC, and we aim to return within three working days. If you report it later than two working days, we will empty the bin on your next normal collection day instead.

Will collections change over Christmas? Yes. There will be no collections on Friday 25 December 2026 or on Friday 1 January 2027. Collections due on 25 December will take place on Sunday 27 December, and collections due on 1 January will take place on Saturday 2 January 2027. Collections on all other days during the holiday period stay on their normal day.

Can I put out extra recycling after Christmas? Yes. Between 21 December 2026 and 8 January 2027, crews will take up to two extra bags of recycling placed beside your blue bin, as long as the bags are clear so the crew can see what is inside. Outside these dates, the normal rule applies and extra bags beside any bin are not collected. Flattened cardboard boxes can also be placed beside the blue bin during this period.

What happens to my real Christmas tree? If you subscribe to the garden waste service, put your tree out next to your green bin on your garden waste day in the two weeks starting Monday 4 January 2027. Trees must be cut into pieces no longer than 1.2 metres, with all decorations, pots and stands removed. Artificial trees are not accepted. Households without a garden waste subscription can take real trees to the Household Recycling Centre at Sandpiper Road free of charge.

How do I join the garden waste service, and what does it cost? You can subscribe online or by phone. The 2026–27 season costs £52 per bin, or £26 per bin for households with a Council Tax Reduction award, and you can have up to three bins. New subscribers receive their green bin within 15 working days.

Can I get a refund if I move? Garden waste refunds are only given if you move out of the district before 30 September. In that case you receive 50% of the price you paid. Moves within the district do not qualify, because the subscription moves with you to your new address.

How do I book a bulky waste collection? Book online or by phone at least five working days in advance. The charge is £35.00 for up to three items and £8.50 for each further item, up to a maximum of eight items. You can cancel free of charge until 12:00 on the working day before the collection. After that time, the charge is not refunded.

How do I get rid of batteries and small electrical items? Put household batteries in a clear sealed bag on top of your blue bin lid. Small electrical items that are no bigger than a shoebox, such as kettles and phone chargers, can be placed in a carrier bag next to your blue bin. Larger electrical items must be booked as bulky waste or taken to Sandpiper Road.

Do you collect glass? No, glass is not collected from the kerbside. Please use one of the 64 bottle banks across the district or take glass to Sandpiper Road.

What can go in my food caddy? All cooked and uncooked food, including meat, fish, bones, tea bags and coffee grounds, can go in the brown caddy. Use only compostable liners, never plastic bags. Each household can collect one free roll of 52 compostable liners per year from any district library, and further rolls cost £3.50 each.

What if my bin is damaged or stolen? Report it online or by phone. A replacement grey or blue bin costs £28 and is delivered within 15 working days. If the damage was caused by one of our crews, the replacement is free. A replacement food caddy is always free of charge.

How do I apply for an assisted collection? Complete form WR-22 online or ask the contact centre to send you a paper copy. An officer may visit to agree a collection point, and a decision is made within 15 working days.

How do I contact Waste Services? Call the Waste Services Contact Centre on 01632 960 418, Monday to Friday, 08:30–17:00. To report dangerous dumped waste, such as needles or chemicals, outside these hours, call 01632 960 999.
