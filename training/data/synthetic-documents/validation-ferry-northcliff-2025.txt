Northcliff Ferries - Passenger Guide (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Northcliff Ferries

Northcliff Ferries serves the local area. Timetables can change in bad weather.

2. The charge for taking a bicycle

A bicycle can be taken on board for 2 euros. Passengers must check in at least 28 minutes before departure. Please keep your ticket until you leave the ship.

3. The luggage allowance

Each passenger may bring up to 22 kg of luggage. A single adult ticket costs 27 euros. Please keep your ticket until you leave the ship.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Northcliff Ferries help desk.
