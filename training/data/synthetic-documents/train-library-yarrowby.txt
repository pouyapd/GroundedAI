Yarrowby Library - Lending Rules (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Yarrowby Library

Yarrowby Library serves the local area. Members can suggest new books at the desk.

2. The fee for a replacement card

A replacement card costs 2 euros. On weekdays the library opens at 08:30. Please handle rare books with care.

3. The loan period for books

Books may be borrowed for 14 days. Printing costs 6 cents per page. Please handle rare books with care.

4. How many items a member may borrow

Members may borrow up to 10 items at a time. The late fee is 29 cents per item per day. Quiet study areas are on the first floor.

5. How many times an item can be renewed

An item can be renewed twice. Please handle rare books with care.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Yarrowby Library help desk.
