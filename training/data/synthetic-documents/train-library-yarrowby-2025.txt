Yarrowby Library - Lending Rules (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Yarrowby Library

Yarrowby Library serves the local area. Please handle rare books with care.

2. The loan period for books

Books may be borrowed for 14 days. On weekdays the library opens at 08:30. Please handle rare books with care.

3. The late fee

The late fee is 29 cents per item per day. An item can be renewed once. Quiet study areas are on the first floor.

4. The price of printing a page

Printing costs 6 cents per page. Members may borrow up to 10 items at a time. Please handle rare books with care.

5. The fee for a replacement card

A replacement card costs 2 euros. Please handle rare books with care.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Yarrowby Library help desk.
