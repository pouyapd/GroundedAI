Westerleigh Library - Lending Rules (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Westerleigh Library

Westerleigh Library serves the local area. Members can suggest new books at the desk.

2. The loan period for books

Books may be borrowed for 27 days. A replacement card costs 4 euros. Members can suggest new books at the desk.

3. The late fee

The late fee is 35 cents per item per day. An item can be renewed three times. Please handle rare books with care.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Westerleigh Library help desk.
