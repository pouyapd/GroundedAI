Fairwyn Museum - Visitor Guide

1. About Fairwyn Museum

Fairwyn Museum serves the local area. Large bags are not allowed in the galleries.

2. The time of last entry

Last entry is at 15:30. Lockers need a deposit of 1 euros. The shop sells books and prints.

3. The price of an adult ticket

An adult ticket costs 24 euros. Children under 5 enter free. Large bags are not allowed in the galleries.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Fairwyn Museum help desk.
