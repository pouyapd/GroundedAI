Oakhaven Library - Lending Rules

1. About Oakhaven Library

Oakhaven Library serves the local area. Quiet study areas are on the first floor.

2. The fee for a replacement card

A replacement card costs 7 euros. An item can be renewed twice. Members can suggest new books at the desk.

3. The loan period for books

Books may be borrowed for 27 days. Members may borrow up to 19 items at a time. Please handle rare books with care.

4. The price of printing a page

Printing costs 13 cents per page. Please handle rare books with care.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Oakhaven Library help desk.
