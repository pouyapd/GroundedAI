Gullstone Harbour Marina – Visitor Berths: Questions and Answers

Last updated: 18 June 2026. This page answers common questions from visiting skippers. Visitors are welcome all year round, and visitor berths are managed by the same marina office as the annual berths.

Where will I be berthed? Most visitors are berthed on pontoon V, which has 40 visitor berths. When pontoon V is full, visitors may be placed on resident berths that are free while their owners are away. The marina can take vessels up to 24 metres in overall length and up to 3.2 metres draught at the lowest tide.

Can I book in advance? Yes. Visitor berths can be booked up to 60 days ahead through the online booking page or by phone. Without a booking, call the marina office on VHF channel 80 when you approach the harbour. The office is open 08:00–20:00 from May to September and 09:00–17:00 from October to April.

How much does a visitor berth cost? From May to September the price is €4.20 per metre per night, and from October to April it is €2.90 per metre per night. Every vessel is charged for at least 8 metres. Multihulls pay 50% more than the normal price. If you stay 7 nights in a row, you pay for only 6.

Can I stop for a few hours only? Yes. A short stay of up to 4 hours between 10:00 and 16:00 costs a flat fee of €12, whatever the size of the vessel. If you stay past 16:00, you are charged the full nightly rate instead.

What is included in the price? Showers and toilets are included, and the access code is given to you when you register. Wi-Fi is free on the network called GHM-Visitor. Electricity is not included and costs a flat €5 per night. The laundry costs €6 per wash and €4 per dry.

What if I arrive when the office is closed? Use the night berth at the end of pontoon V and register at the office by 09:30 the next morning. If the night berth is taken, raft up alongside another visitor and leave fenders out on both sides.

When do I have to leave? Departure time is 12:00 noon. If you leave after 14:00, you are charged for another night. Please return your key card to the office when you leave, because a lost key card costs €20.

Can I cancel a booking? Bookings cancelled more than 72 hours before the arrival date are free of charge. If you cancel later, or do not arrive, you are charged for one night.

Are there discounts for groups? Groups of 6 or more boats that book together receive 10% off visitor berth fees. Cruising clubs and rallies should contact the Events Coordinator, Mateus Sandoval-Obi, at least 4 weeks before arrival.

Can I get fuel and use the pump-out? The fuel pontoon is open 08:30–18:00 every day from April to October. The black water pump-out at the end of pontoon D costs €15 per use for visitors.

Are there any restrictions on entering the harbour? Vessels with a draught of more than 2.5 metres should not enter or leave within 2 hours either side of low water. The speed limit is 3 knots inside the marina. When winds of force 7 or more are forecast, the office may ask visitors on the outer end of pontoon V to move to the inner pontoons.

Can I leave my boat here and go home for a while? Yes. Visitors may leave their boat unattended for up to 14 nights, as long as the office has the phone number of a keyholder. Visitors who want to stay longer than 28 nights between October and April can apply for a temporary monthly berth, which costs €95 per metre per month. Temporary monthly berths are not available from May to September.

Where can I park my car? Visitors can park one car in the visitor car park for €8 per day, for up to 7 days. Car park tickets are sold at the marina office. The berth holders' car park is for permit holders only.

Is there a shop nearby? The marina chandlery next to the office is open 09:00–17:00 every day except Tuesday and sells charts, gas refills and basic food. Visitors receive 5% off chandlery purchases when they show their berth receipt.

Can I bring my dog? Yes, dogs are welcome on board and ashore. They must be kept on a lead on the pontoons and in the car park.
