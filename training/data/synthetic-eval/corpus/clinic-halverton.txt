Halverton Health Centre - Patient Information (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Halverton Health Centre

Halverton Health Centre serves the local area. Interpreters can be arranged.

2. How far ahead appointments can be booked

Appointments can be booked up to 6 weeks ahead. The phone line opens at 07:00. The waiting room has step-free access.

3. The cancellation notice for appointments

Please cancel at least 42 hours before your appointment. A missed appointment costs 37 euros. The waiting room has step-free access.

4. How long a repeat prescription takes

Repeat prescriptions are ready within 2 working days. Please bring a list of your medicines.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Halverton Health Centre help desk.
