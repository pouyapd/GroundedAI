Dunmore College - Admissions Information (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Dunmore College

Dunmore College serves the local area. Advisers can help with course choices.

2. The yearly tuition fee

The tuition fee is 6874 euros per year. Students must attend at least 90% of classes. Advisers can help with course choices.

3. The application deadline

Applications must arrive by 13 May. The library is open to all students.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Dunmore College help desk.
