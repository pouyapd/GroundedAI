Pembury Ferries - Passenger Guide (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Pembury Ferries

Pembury Ferries serves the local area. Timetables can change in bad weather.

2. The time of the first departure

The first ferry leaves at 07:30 every day. Each passenger may bring up to 20 kg of luggage. Please keep your ticket until you leave the ship.

3. The price of a single adult ticket

A single adult ticket costs 23 euros. A bicycle can be taken on board for 5 euros. Timetables can change in bad weather.

4. The refund deadline

Tickets can be refunded up to 42 hours before departure. Passengers must check in at least 22 minutes before departure. Our crews are trained in first aid.

5. The fare for children

Children aged 5 to 15 pay 3 euros. Timetables can change in bad weather.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Pembury Ferries help desk.
