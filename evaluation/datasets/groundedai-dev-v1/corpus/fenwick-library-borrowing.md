# Fenwick Public Library - Borrowing Rules

## Loans
Books can be borrowed for 21 days. DVDs and video games can be borrowed for 7 days. Each adult member may have up to 12 items on loan at the same time; children's cards are limited to 8 items.

## Renewals
Items can be renewed up to three times online or at the desk, unless another member has reserved them.

## Overdue items
Children are never charged overdue fines. Adults pay 20p per item for each day an item is overdue, up to a maximum of £6 per item.

## Membership cards
Membership is free for anyone who lives, works or studies in Fenwick. A replacement for a lost card costs £2.50.
