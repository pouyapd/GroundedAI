Aldervale Systems Leave and Holiday Policy
Effective 1 January 2025.

Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 28 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Leave is requested in the HR portal. Requests for more than three consecutive days should be made at least two weeks in advance, so that the team can plan cover.

Carry-over of unused leave

Up to five unused leave days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March; days not taken by then expire. Exceptions are only possible when leave could not be taken because of long-term illness or parental leave.

Sick leave

Employees who are ill must inform their line manager by 09:30 on the first day of absence. A doctor's note is required from the third day of illness. Sick days during booked annual leave are treated as sick leave when a doctor's note is provided, and the annual leave days are returned.

Parental leave

Aldervale tops up statutory parental pay to 80 percent of the normal salary for the first eight weeks of parental leave. Employees should inform HR at least eight weeks before the planned start of parental leave.

Volunteering day

Every employee can take one paid volunteering day per year to support a registered charity or community project. The volunteering day is requested in the HR portal like annual leave.

Special leave

Paid special leave of up to two days is granted for moving house, for the wedding of the employee, and for the death of a close family member. Special leave is approved by the line manager.
