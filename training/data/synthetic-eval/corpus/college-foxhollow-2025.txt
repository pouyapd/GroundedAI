Foxhollow College - Admissions Information (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Foxhollow College

Foxhollow College serves the local area. Open days are held every term.

2. The deposit to accept a place

A deposit of 214 euros secures a place. Students must attend at least 90% of classes. Advisers can help with course choices.

3. The application deadline

Applications must arrive by 14 April. The tuition fee is 2513 euros per year. Advisers can help with course choices.

4. The maximum class size

Classes have at most 29 students. The library is open to all students.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Foxhollow College help desk.
