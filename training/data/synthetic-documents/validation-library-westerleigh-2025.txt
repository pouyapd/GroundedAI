Westerleigh Library - Lending Rules (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Westerleigh Library

Westerleigh Library serves the local area. Members can suggest new books at the desk.

2. How many times an item can be renewed

An item can be renewed three times. A replacement card costs 4 euros. Quiet study areas are on the first floor.

3. The late fee

The late fee is 35 cents per item per day. Books may be borrowed for 25 days. Please handle rare books with care.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Westerleigh Library help desk.
