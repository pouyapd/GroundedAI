Aldervale Systems Finance FAQ
Last updated May 2025. Maintained by the Finance Help Team.

How long does it take to get my expenses reimbursed?
Approved expense claims are paid with the next payment run. Reimbursements are paid within 10 working days after the claim has been approved by the line manager.

What is the nightly hotel limit?
The nightly hotel limit is €120 in all cities. Bookings above this amount must be explained in the comment field of the claim.

Which tool do I use for expense claims?
All claims are entered in LedgerLeaf, the company expense tool. Photos of receipts can be uploaded from the LedgerLeaf mobile app.

What if I lost a receipt?
If a receipt is lost, complete the lost receipt declaration form FIN-07. The form can be used for expenses of up to €50 per claim. Lost receipts for higher amounts cannot be reimbursed.

How are expenses in foreign currencies converted?
When an expense was not paid with a company card, Finance converts it using the European Central Bank reference rate of the transaction date.

Can I get an advance for a long trip?
Employees travelling for more than two weeks can request a cash advance of up to €500. The advance is deducted from the next expense claim.

Who approves training costs?
Training and conference fees are approved by the line manager when they are below €1,000. Higher amounts need approval from the head of department and the learning team.

How do I report a payment problem?
Open a ticket in the internal service desk and choose the category "Finance - Payments". The Finance Help Team answers tickets within two working days.
