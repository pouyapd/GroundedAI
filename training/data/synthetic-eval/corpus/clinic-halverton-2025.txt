Halverton Health Centre - Patient Information (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Halverton Health Centre

Halverton Health Centre serves the local area. The waiting room has step-free access.

2. The fee for a missed appointment

A missed appointment costs 21 euros. The phone line opens at 07:00. Please bring a list of your medicines.

3. How long a repeat prescription takes

Repeat prescriptions are ready within 2 working days. Appointments can be booked up to 6 weeks ahead. Please bring a list of your medicines.

4. The cancellation notice for appointments

Please cancel at least 42 hours before your appointment. Interpreters can be arranged.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Halverton Health Centre help desk.
