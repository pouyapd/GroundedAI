Greyhallow Fitness - Membership Terms (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Greyhallow Fitness

Greyhallow Fitness serves the local area. Staff can show you how to use the machines.

2. The cancellation notice period

Memberships can be cancelled with 48 days notice. A guest pass costs 9 euros per visit. Staff can show you how to use the machines.

3. The monthly membership fee

The monthly membership fee is 39 euros. Classes can be booked up to 13 days in advance. Staff can show you how to use the machines.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Greyhallow Fitness help desk.
