Halden Coworking - Membership Guide

Plans
A hot desk membership costs £180 per month and gives access on weekdays from 08:00 to 20:00. A fixed desk membership costs £260 per month and includes 24-hour access, seven days a week.

Meeting rooms
Fixed desk members get 4 hours of meeting-room time per month and hot desk members get 2 hours. Extra time costs £15 per hour.

Guests
Members may bring up to two guests per day at no charge. Guests must sign in at reception.

Cancelling
Memberships can be cancelled with one calendar month's notice, given in writing to the community manager.

Mail
Fixed desk members can use Halden as their business address; mail is held at reception for 14 days.
