CORVANE SYSTEMS - ACCESS CONTROL STANDARD CS-AC-01
Revision A. Approved November 2018.

1 Scope
This standard applies to every system operated by Corvane Systems. It defines two assurance levels: AL1 for internal tools and AL2 for systems that hold customer data.

2 Passwords
Passwords SHALL be at least 10 characters long.
Accounts SHALL be locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts.
Passwords SHALL NOT be stored in plain text.

3 Sessions
At AL1, users SHALL be reauthenticated after 2 hours of inactivity.
At AL2, users SHALL be reauthenticated after 30 minutes of inactivity.
At AL2, sessions SHALL be reauthenticated at least once every 8 hours, regardless of activity.

4 Administrators
Administrators SHOULD NOT share accounts.
Administrator access SHALL be reviewed every 6 months.
