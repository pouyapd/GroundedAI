Corrindale Library - Lending Rules

1. About Corrindale Library

Corrindale Library serves the local area. Please handle rare books with care.

2. The fee for a replacement card

A replacement card costs 5 euros. Books may be borrowed for 15 days. Quiet study areas are on the first floor.

3. How many times an item can be renewed

An item can be renewed once. On weekdays the library opens at 09:00. Members can suggest new books at the desk.

4. How many items a member may borrow

Members may borrow up to 13 items at a time. Members can suggest new books at the desk.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Corrindale Library help desk.
