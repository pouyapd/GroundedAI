Dunmore College - Admissions Information (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Dunmore College

Dunmore College serves the local area. Advisers can help with course choices.

2. The application deadline

Applications must arrive by 13 May. Students must attend at least 90% of classes. Open days are held every term.

3. The yearly tuition fee

The tuition fee is 7322 euros per year. Open days are held every term.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Dunmore College help desk.
