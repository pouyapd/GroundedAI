Rosedown College - Admissions Information

1. About Rosedown College

Rosedown College serves the local area. Advisers can help with course choices.

2. The deposit to accept a place

A deposit of 431 euros secures a place. Applications must arrive by 26 May. Advisers can help with course choices.

3. The yearly tuition fee

The tuition fee is 5428 euros per year. Advisers can help with course choices.

Contact
