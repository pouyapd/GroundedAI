Brindlewick Cycling Club - Ride Rules for Members

General rules

Helmets are mandatory on all club rides. Riders must follow the traffic rules at all times and must have working lights from sunset to sunrise. Mudguards are recommended between October and March so that riders behind stay dry.

Group rides

Club group rides start every Saturday at 08:00 from the Old Mill car park. Riders choose one of three speed groups. Group A rides at an average of more than 30 km/h. Group B rides at 25 to 28 km/h. Group C rides at 20 to 23 km/h and follows a no-drop policy, which means the group waits for every rider. New members are asked to start with group C for their first two rides.

Weather and cancellations

Rides are cancelled when the temperature is below -2 °C or when the national weather service issues an ice warning for the region. Cancellations are posted in the club chat by 07:00 on the day of the ride.

Membership

The annual membership fee is €60 for adults and €20 for juniors under 18. Membership includes third-party insurance through the national cycling federation. Guests may join two rides before they need to become members.

Club kit

Club jerseys can be ordered twice a year, in February and in August. Wearing club kit is welcome but not required.
