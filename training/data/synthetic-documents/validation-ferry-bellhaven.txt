Bellhaven Ferries - Passenger Guide

1. About Bellhaven Ferries

Bellhaven Ferries serves the local area. Our crews are trained in first aid.

2. The price of a single adult ticket

A single adult ticket costs 26 euros. Passengers must check in at least 59 minutes before departure. Please keep your ticket until you leave the ship.

3. The refund deadline

Tickets can be refunded up to 28 hours before departure. Each passenger may bring up to 31 kg of luggage. Our crews are trained in first aid.

4. The charge for taking a bicycle

A bicycle can be taken on board for 3 euros. Please keep your ticket until you leave the ship.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Bellhaven Ferries help desk.
