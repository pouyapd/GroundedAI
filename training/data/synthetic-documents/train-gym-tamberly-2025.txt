Tamberly Fitness - Membership Terms (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Tamberly Fitness

Tamberly Fitness serves the local area. Lockers are emptied every night.

2. The price of a guest pass

A guest pass costs 16 euros per visit. The monthly membership fee is 45 euros. Lockers are emptied every night.

3. The weekday opening time

On weekdays the gym opens at 06:00. Classes can be booked up to 12 days in advance. Please wipe equipment after use.

4. The cancellation notice period

Memberships can be cancelled with 20 days notice. Please wipe equipment after use.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Tamberly Fitness help desk.
