# Aldervale Remote Work Guidelines

Version 2.1, January 2025. Owner: People Team.

## Who can work remotely

Employees whose role does not require them to be on site can work remotely after their probation period. Employees may work remotely for up to three days per week. Teams agree on at least one shared office day each week so that planning and onboarding can happen in person.

## Working hours

Remote employees keep the same working hours as in the office. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 CET; during core hours employees should be reachable by chat and phone. Outside core hours employees can organise their time flexibly, as long as the agreed weekly hours are met.

## Home office equipment and allowances

Every remote employee receives a laptop, a monitor and a headset. New remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of €300 for furniture such as a desk chair. In addition, the company pays €25 per month towards the cost of a home internet connection. Equipment remains the property of Aldervale and must be returned when employment ends.

## Working from abroad

Working from another country is possible for up to 20 working days per calendar year. It needs approval from HR at least four weeks in advance, because tax and insurance rules have to be checked for each country.

## Security when working remotely

The company VPN must be used for all access to internal systems. Public Wi-Fi networks, for example in cafés or hotels, may only be used together with the VPN. Screens must not be visible to other people when confidential information is shown. Remember to change your password every 90 days.

## Getting help

The IT service desk is available from 08:00 to 18:00 CET on working days. Hardware faults are handled by sending a replacement device by courier.
