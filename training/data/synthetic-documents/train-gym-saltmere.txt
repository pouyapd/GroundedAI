Saltmere Fitness - Membership Terms

1. About Saltmere Fitness

Saltmere Fitness serves the local area. Lockers are emptied every night.

2. The weekday opening time

On weekdays the gym opens at 05:00. The monthly membership fee is 38 euros. Please wipe equipment after use.

3. How far ahead classes can be booked

Classes can be booked up to 14 days in advance. New members pay a one-time joining fee of 12 euros. Lockers are emptied every night.

4. The cancellation notice period

Memberships can be cancelled with 47 days notice. A guest pass costs 7 euros per visit. Please wipe equipment after use.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Saltmere Fitness help desk.
