Willowmere Water - Customer Service Standards

1. About Willowmere Water

Willowmere Water serves the local area. Our call centre can arrange a visit.

2. The compensation for a missed appointment

If we miss an appointment we pay 36 pounds in compensation. Written complaints are answered within 17 working days. Please report leaks as soon as you see them.

3. The reconnection fee

The reconnection fee is 36 pounds. We attend reported leaks within 8 hours. Please report leaks as soon as you see them.
