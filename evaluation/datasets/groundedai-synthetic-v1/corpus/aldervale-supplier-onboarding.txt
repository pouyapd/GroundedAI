Aldervale Procurement: Supplier Onboarding Notes
Internal working notes, collected by the procurement team in June 2025.

Before the first order

New suppliers must provide their VAT identification number and their bank details on company letterhead. Procurement also needs a signed copy of the Aldervale supplier code of conduct. Onboarding usually takes about 10 business days after all documents have been received.

Payment terms

The standard payment terms for suppliers are net 45 days from the date of a correct invoice. Early payment discounts can be agreed in the contract, but only Finance can approve them. Invoices must quote the purchase order number, otherwise they are returned to the supplier.

Supplier reviews

Preferred suppliers are reviewed once a year in November. The review looks at delivery times, quality complaints and pricing. Suppliers with repeated quality complaints are moved to a watch list and may lose preferred status.

Contacts

Questions about onboarding go to the procurement mailbox. Contract questions are handled by the legal team.
