1 About these standards
This document sets out the service that Kestrel Valley Water gives to its customers in the Kestrel Valley supply area. It replaces the standards published in 2015. The standards apply to residential and business customers alike unless a section says otherwise.
Kestrel Valley Water supplies drinking water to about 210,000 households and 9,000 businesses through 3,400 kilometres of mains.

2 Leaks and supply problems
We SHALL attend reported leaks within 4 hours of the first report, day or night.
Customers are given at least 48 hours notice of a service interruption for planned work on the mains.
If an unplanned interruption lasts longer than 12 hours, we deliver bottled water to customers who ask for it.
The emergency helpline answers calls within 2 minutes on average.

3 Meters and bills
Meters are read every 6 months. Customers may also send their own reading through the website.
Bills are issued twice a year, after each meter reading.
The reconnection fee is GBP 45 when a supply has been disconnected at the customer's request.
Customers who pay by direct debit may spread the cost over 10 monthly payments.

4 Visits to your property
Field staff SHOULD show photo identification before entering a property.
Appointments are offered in morning or afternoon slots of 4 hours.
If we miss an appointment without 24 hours notice, the customer receives a payment of GBP 20.

5 Complaints
We reply to written complaints within 10 working days.
A customer who is not satisfied with the reply can ask for a review by a senior manager.
Contact: Kestrel Valley Water, Customer Relations, Mill Road, Kestrelford.
