Foxhollow College - Admissions Information (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Foxhollow College

Foxhollow College serves the local area. Open days are held every term.

2. The attendance requirement

Students must attend at least 90% of classes. A deposit of 214 euros secures a place. The library is open to all students.

3. The yearly tuition fee

The tuition fee is 2513 euros per year. Applications must arrive by 18 April. Advisers can help with course choices.

4. The maximum class size

Classes have at most 29 students. Open days are held every term.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Foxhollow College help desk.
