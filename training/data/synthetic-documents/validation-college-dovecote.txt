Dovecote College - Admissions Information

1. About Dovecote College

Dovecote College serves the local area. Advisers can help with course choices.

2. The yearly tuition fee

The tuition fee is 6956 euros per year. Students must attend at least 80% of classes. Open days are held every term.

3. The maximum class size

Classes have at most 22 students. A deposit of 493 euros secures a place. Open days are held every term.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Dovecote College help desk.
