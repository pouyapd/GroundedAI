Cindermoor Fitness - Membership Terms

1. About Cindermoor Fitness

Cindermoor Fitness serves the local area. Please wipe equipment after use.

2. The weekday opening time

On weekdays the gym opens at 07:30. The monthly membership fee is 54 euros. Please wipe equipment after use.

3. The joining fee

New members pay a one-time joining fee of 48 euros. Classes can be booked up to 13 days in advance. Please wipe equipment after use.

4. The price of a guest pass

A guest pass costs 17 euros per visit. Memberships can be cancelled with 16 days notice. Lockers are emptied every night.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Cindermoor Fitness help desk.
