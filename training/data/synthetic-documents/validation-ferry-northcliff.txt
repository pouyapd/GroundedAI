Northcliff Ferries - Passenger Guide (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Northcliff Ferries

Northcliff Ferries serves the local area. Our crews are trained in first aid.

2. The luggage allowance

Each passenger may bring up to 18 kg of luggage. A bicycle can be taken on board for 2 euros. Please keep your ticket until you leave the ship.

3. How early passengers must check in

Passengers must check in at least 28 minutes before departure. A single adult ticket costs 27 euros. Please keep your ticket until you leave the ship.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Northcliff Ferries help desk.
