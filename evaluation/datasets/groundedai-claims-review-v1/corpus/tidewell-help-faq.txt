Tidewell Bike Share - Help Centre FAQ (2025 edition)

How much is an annual pass?
For 2025, the annual pass is £84. It covers unlimited rides of up to 45 minutes.

How long can I keep a bike before extra charges start?
You can ride for 30 minutes on any pass before extra time is charged. After that, every additional 15 minutes costs £1.

Can I take a bike on the train?
Tidewell bikes may not be taken on trains or buses, because they must stay within the service area.

Do I need a helmet?
Helmets are recommended but not required. Tidewell does not provide helmets.

What happens if I lose my membership card?
You can unlock bikes with the app instead. A replacement card costs £3 and arrives within five working days.

Can I reserve a bike?
Yes. In the app, a bike can be reserved for up to 10 minutes before you reach the station. Reservations are free, but you can only hold one reservation at a time.
