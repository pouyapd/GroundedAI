Ashcombe Ridge Ferries - Passenger Guide

1. About Ashcombe Ridge Ferries

Ashcombe Ridge Ferries serves the local area. Please keep your ticket until you leave the ship.

2. The luggage allowance

Each passenger may bring up to 37 kg of luggage. Tickets can be refunded up to 48 hours before departure. Timetables can change in bad weather.

3. The charge for taking a bicycle

A bicycle can be taken on board for 7 euros. Children aged 5 to 15 pay 8 euros. Timetables can change in bad weather.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Ashcombe Ridge Ferries help desk.
