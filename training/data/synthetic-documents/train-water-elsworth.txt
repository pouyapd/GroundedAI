Elsworth Water - Customer Service Standards

1. About Elsworth Water

Elsworth Water serves the local area. We test water quality every day.

2. How quickly reported leaks are attended

We attend reported leaks within 5 hours. If we miss an appointment we pay 40 pounds in compensation. Please report leaks as soon as you see them.

3. How often meters are read

Meters are read every 12 months. The reconnection fee is 50 pounds. Our call centre can arrange a visit.
