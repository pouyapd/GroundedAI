Eastwold Ferries - Passenger Guide

1. About Eastwold Ferries

Eastwold Ferries serves the local area. Our crews are trained in first aid.

2. The refund deadline

Tickets can be refunded up to 4 hours before departure. A bicycle can be taken on board for 6 euros. Our crews are trained in first aid.

3. The fare for children

Children aged 5 to 15 pay 3 euros. Passengers must check in at least 15 minutes before departure. Timetables can change in bad weather.

4. The price of a single adult ticket

A single adult ticket costs 11 euros. Each passenger may bring up to 32 kg of luggage. Please keep your ticket until you leave the ship.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Eastwold Ferries help desk.
