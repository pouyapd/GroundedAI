Fernhollow Museum - Visitor Guide (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Fernhollow Museum

Fernhollow Museum serves the local area. The shop sells books and prints.

2. The age under which children enter free

Children under 16 enter free. An adult ticket costs 12 euros. The shop sells books and prints.

3. The time of last entry

Last entry is at 15:00. Photography without flash is allowed.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Fernhollow Museum help desk.
