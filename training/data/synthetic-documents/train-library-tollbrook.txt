Tollbrook Library - Lending Rules

1. About Tollbrook Library

Tollbrook Library serves the local area. Please handle rare books with care.

2. The weekday opening time

On weekdays the library opens at 09:00. A replacement card costs 4 euros. Members can suggest new books at the desk.

3. How many items a member may borrow

Members may borrow up to 12 items at a time. Printing costs 18 cents per page. Quiet study areas are on the first floor.

4. The loan period for books

Books may be borrowed for 23 days. Quiet study areas are on the first floor.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Tollbrook Library help desk.
