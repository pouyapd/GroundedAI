Amberleigh Ferries - Passenger Guide

1. About Amberleigh Ferries

Amberleigh Ferries serves the local area. Timetables can change in bad weather.

2. The time of the first departure

The first ferry leaves at 07:00 every day. Passengers must check in at least 56 minutes before departure. Timetables can change in bad weather.

3. The charge for taking a bicycle

A bicycle can be taken on board for 8 euros. Each passenger may bring up to 37 kg of luggage. Our crews are trained in first aid.

4. The price of a single adult ticket

A single adult ticket costs 11 euros. Tickets can be refunded up to 32 hours before departure. Timetables can change in bad weather.

5. The fare for children

Children aged 5 to 15 pay 12 euros. Our crews are trained in first aid.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Amberleigh Ferries help desk.
