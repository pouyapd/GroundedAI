Kestrel Valley Rail - Delay Compensation and Refunds

This policy applies to all journeys on Kestrel Valley Rail services from 1 January 2026.

Delay compensation
If your train arrives at your destination 30 to 59 minutes late, you can claim 25% of the single fare. For delays of 60 to 119 minutes the compensation is 50% of the single fare. For delays of 120 minutes or more you receive the full single fare back. Claims must be made within 28 days of the date of travel. Season ticket holders claim per delayed journey, based on the daily cost of their ticket.

Refunds
Unused Anytime tickets can be refunded within 28 days of the travel date, minus a £5 administration fee. Advance tickets cannot be refunded, but the travel date can be changed before departure for a fee of £10.

Bicycles
Bicycles travel free of charge, but a space must be reserved at least two hours before departure. Bicycles are not allowed on trains that arrive in Kestrel Central between 07:00 and 09:30 on weekdays.

Assistance
Passengers who need help boarding can book assistance up to two hours before travelling by calling the Passenger Assist line.
