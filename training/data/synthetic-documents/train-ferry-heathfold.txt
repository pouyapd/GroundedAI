Heathfold Ferries - Passenger Guide

1. About Heathfold Ferries

Heathfold Ferries serves the local area. Our crews are trained in first aid.

2. The charge for taking a bicycle

A bicycle can be taken on board for 5 euros. Each passenger may bring up to 30 kg of luggage. Please keep your ticket until you leave the ship.

3. The price of a single adult ticket

A single adult ticket costs 27 euros. Tickets can be refunded up to 17 hours before departure. Our crews are trained in first aid.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Heathfold Ferries help desk.
