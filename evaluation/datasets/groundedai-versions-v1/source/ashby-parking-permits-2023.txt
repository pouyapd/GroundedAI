ASHBY COUNCIL - RESIDENTS' PARKING PERMIT SCHEME
Scheme rules 2023. Issued June 2023.

1 Who can apply
Residents of a controlled parking zone can apply for a residents' permit for one vehicle registered at their address.
Applicants MUST provide proof of address and the vehicle's registration document.

2 Prices
A residents' permit costs GBP 110 per year.
Blue Badge holders receive a residents' permit free of charge.
Fully electric vehicles receive a 50% discount on the permit price.

3 Validity
A residents' permit is valid for one year from the date of issue.
Permits are valid only in the zone printed on them.

4 Visitors
Each household can buy up to 30 visitor permits per year.
A visitor permit is valid for one day.

5 Enforcement
Vehicles parked without a valid permit can receive a penalty charge notice of GBP 70.
The penalty is reduced by half if it is paid within 14 days.
