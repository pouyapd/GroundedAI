Quillon Software - Support Policy

Response times
On the Standard plan, support requests receive a first response within 2 business days. On the Priority plan, the first response arrives within 4 business hours, and critical incidents are handled around the clock.

Supported versions
Quillon fixes security issues in the current major version and the previous major version. Version 7 reaches end of support on 31 December 2027.

Data export
Customers can export all their data in CSV or JSON format from the Settings page at any time, including after cancelling, for 60 days.

Refunds
Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase.
