Tidewell Bike Share - Member Terms (valid from 1 March 2026)

Passes
An annual pass costs £96 and includes unlimited rides of up to 45 minutes each. A monthly pass costs £12. A day pass costs £4 and can be bought at any docking station with a contactless card. Students with a valid university card get 25% off the annual pass.

Ride time and extra charges
If a ride lasts longer than 45 minutes, each additional 15 minutes costs £1. Electric bikes carry a surcharge of 10p per minute on every pass. Riders must be at least 16 years old; riders aged 16 or 17 need a parent or guardian to accept these terms on their behalf.

Returning bikes
Bikes must be returned to a Tidewell docking station. A bike left outside a docking station is charged a recovery fee of £25. If a station is full, the app shows the nearest station with a free dock and adds 15 minutes of free ride time.

Damage and theft
Report damage within 24 hours through the app. Members are not charged for normal wear. A stolen bike must be reported to the police and to Tidewell within 48 hours; if both reports are made in time, the member pays nothing for the bike.

Cancelling
A monthly pass can be cancelled at any time and ends at the close of the month already paid. An annual pass can be cancelled within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. After 14 days, annual passes are not refunded.
