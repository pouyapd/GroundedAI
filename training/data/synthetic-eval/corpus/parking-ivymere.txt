Ivymere Council - Residents' Parking Permits (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Ivymere Council

Ivymere Council serves the local area. Enforcement officers patrol every day.

2. How long an application takes

Applications are processed within 8 working days. A household may hold at most 2 permits. Enforcement officers patrol every day.

3. The price of an annual permit

An annual resident permit costs 57 euros. A permit for a second car costs 200 euros. Changes of address must be reported.

4. How many visitor permits a household gets

Each household may receive 66 visitor permits per year. Permits must be displayed in the windscreen.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Ivymere Council help desk.
