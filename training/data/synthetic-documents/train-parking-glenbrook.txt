Glenbrook Council - Residents' Parking Permits

1. About Glenbrook Council

Glenbrook Council serves the local area. Permits must be displayed in the windscreen.

2. How long an application takes

Applications are processed within 3 working days. Each household may receive 51 visitor permits per year. Enforcement officers patrol every day.

3. The price of a permit for a second car

A permit for a second car costs 163 euros. Permits must be displayed in the windscreen.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Glenbrook Council help desk.
