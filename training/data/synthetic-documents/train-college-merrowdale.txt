Merrowdale College - Admissions Information

1. About Merrowdale College

Merrowdale College serves the local area. Open days are held every term.

2. The application deadline

Applications must arrive by 10 June. Students must attend at least 80% of classes. Advisers can help with course choices.

3. The deposit to accept a place

A deposit of 144 euros secures a place. Open days are held every term.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Merrowdale College help desk.
