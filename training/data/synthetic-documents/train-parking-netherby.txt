Netherby Council - Residents' Parking Permits (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Netherby Council

Netherby Council serves the local area. Permits must be displayed in the windscreen.

2. The number of permits per household

A household may hold at most 3 permits. A permit for a second car costs 155 euros. Enforcement officers patrol every day.

3. The price of an annual permit

An annual resident permit costs 55 euros. Permits must be displayed in the windscreen.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Netherby Council help desk.
