Kestrel Valley Rail - Passenger FAQ (2024 edition)

How do I claim for a delay?
Use the online Delay Repay form. Claims must be submitted within 14 days of the date of travel, and you will need your ticket number.

Can I take my dog on the train?
Dogs travel free of charge. Each passenger may bring up to two dogs, which must be kept on a lead or in a carrier.

Is there Wi-Fi on board?
Free Wi-Fi is available on all trains, limited to 50 MB per journey.

Where can I find lost property?
Items found on trains are kept at Kestrel Central station for 30 days.
