Copperfield Makerspace - Workshop Safety Rules

Wood shop
Safety glasses must be worn at all times in the wood shop. Loose clothing and long hair must be tied back before using the table saw or the lathe. Nobody may use the table saw alone: a second member must be in the room.

Laser cutter
No induction is needed to use the laser cutter; members only have to read the safety sheet next to the machine. Never leave the laser cutter running unattended. Do not cut PVC or vinyl, which release toxic gas.

Soldering
Use the fume extractor whenever you solder. Lead-free solder is provided free of charge.

Children
Children under 14 may visit the makerspace with a parent but may not use any power tools. Members aged 14 to 17 may use power tools only under the direct supervision of a staff member.

Accidents
Report every accident, however small, to the duty manager on the same day. First-aid kits are next to the main door and in the wood shop.
