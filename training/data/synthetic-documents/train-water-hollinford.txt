Hollinford Water - Customer Service Standards (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Hollinford Water

Hollinford Water serves the local area. Our call centre can arrange a visit.

2. How quickly reported leaks are attended

We attend reported leaks within 4 hours. Written complaints are answered within 12 working days. Please report leaks as soon as you see them.

3. The compensation for a missed appointment

If we miss an appointment we pay 21 pounds in compensation. The reconnection fee is 55 pounds. Our call centre can arrange a visit.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Hollinford Water help desk.
