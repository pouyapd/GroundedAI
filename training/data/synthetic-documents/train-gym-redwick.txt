Redwick Fitness - Membership Terms

1. About Redwick Fitness

Redwick Fitness serves the local area. Please wipe equipment after use.

2. The monthly membership fee

The monthly membership fee is 37 euros. Memberships can be cancelled with 40 days notice. Staff can show you how to use the machines.

3. The joining fee

New members pay a one-time joining fee of 36 euros. Lockers are emptied every night.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Redwick Fitness help desk.
