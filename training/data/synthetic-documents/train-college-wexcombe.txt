Wexcombe College - Admissions Information

1. About Wexcombe College

Wexcombe College serves the local area. The library is open to all students.

2. The deposit to accept a place

A deposit of 119 euros secures a place. Students must attend at least 70% of classes. Advisers can help with course choices.

3. The maximum class size

Classes have at most 17 students. Applications must arrive by 9 May. Advisers can help with course choices.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Wexcombe College help desk.
