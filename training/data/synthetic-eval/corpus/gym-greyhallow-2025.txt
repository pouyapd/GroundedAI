Greyhallow Fitness - Membership Terms (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Greyhallow Fitness

Greyhallow Fitness serves the local area. Please wipe equipment after use.

2. The price of a guest pass

A guest pass costs 9 euros per visit. Classes can be booked up to 13 days in advance. Staff can show you how to use the machines.

3. The monthly membership fee

The monthly membership fee is 31 euros. Memberships can be cancelled with 48 days notice. Lockers are emptied every night.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Greyhallow Fitness help desk.
