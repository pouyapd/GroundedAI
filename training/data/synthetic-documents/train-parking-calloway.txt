Calloway Council - Residents' Parking Permits

1. About Calloway Council

Calloway Council serves the local area. Changes of address must be reported.

2. The price of an annual permit

An annual resident permit costs 100 euros. A permit for a second car costs 177 euros. Enforcement officers patrol every day.

3. How many visitor permits a household gets

Each household may receive 51 visitor permits per year. Applications are processed within 7 working days. Enforcement officers patrol every day.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Calloway Council help desk.
