Orrinton Library - Lending Rules

1. About Orrinton Library

Orrinton Library serves the local area. Please handle rare books with care.

2. How many times an item can be renewed

An item can be renewed three times. Members may borrow up to 8 items at a time. Please handle rare books with care.

3. The fee for a replacement card

A replacement card costs 6 euros. Printing costs 11 cents per page. Please handle rare books with care.

4. The weekday opening time

On weekdays the library opens at 08:00. Quiet study areas are on the first floor.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Orrinton Library help desk.
