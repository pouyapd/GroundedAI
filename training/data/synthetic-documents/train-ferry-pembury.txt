Pembury Ferries - Passenger Guide (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Pembury Ferries

Pembury Ferries serves the local area. Timetables can change in bad weather.

2. The time of the first departure

The first ferry leaves at 07:30 every day. Tickets can be refunded up to 42 hours before departure. Our crews are trained in first aid.

3. The charge for taking a bicycle

A bicycle can be taken on board for 8 euros. Passengers must check in at least 22 minutes before departure. Our crews are trained in first aid.

4. The luggage allowance

Each passenger may bring up to 20 kg of luggage. A single adult ticket costs 23 euros. Please keep your ticket until you leave the ship.

5. The fare for children

Children aged 5 to 15 pay 3 euros. Our crews are trained in first aid.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Pembury Ferries help desk.
