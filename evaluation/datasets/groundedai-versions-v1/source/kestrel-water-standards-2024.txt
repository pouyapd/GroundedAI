1 About these standards
This document sets out the service that Kestrel Valley Water gives to its customers in the Kestrel Valley supply area. It replaces the edition published in March 2019. The standards apply to residential and business customers alike unless a section says otherwise.
Kestrel Valley Water now supplies drinking water to about 226,000 households and 9,400 businesses.

2 Leaks and supply problems
We SHALL attend reported leaks within 2 hours of the first report, day or night.
Customers are given at least 72 hours notice of a planned outage.
If an unplanned interruption lasts longer than 12 hours, we deliver bottled water to customers who ask for it.
The emergency helpline answers calls within 120 seconds on average.

3 Meters and bills
Meters are read every three months. Smart meters send a reading every day.
Bills are issued four times a year.
A reconnection charge of GBP 60 applies when a supply has been disconnected at the customer's request.
Residential customers receive a water quality report every 12 months; business customers receive one every 6 months.

4 Visits to your property
Field staff SHALL show photo identification before entering a property.
Appointments are offered in two-hour slots between 08:00 and 18:00.
If we miss an appointment without 24 hours notice, the customer receives a payment of GBP 30.

5 Complaints
Written complaints receive a reply within ten working days.
A customer who is not satisfied with the reply can ask the Consumer Council for Water to review it.
Contact: Kestrel Valley Water, Customer Relations, Mill Road, Kestrelford.
