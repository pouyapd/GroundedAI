Netherby Council - Residents' Parking Permits (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Netherby Council

Netherby Council serves the local area. Changes of address must be reported.

2. The price of a permit for a second car

A permit for a second car costs 84 euros. A household may hold at most 3 permits. Permits must be displayed in the windscreen.

3. The price of an annual permit

An annual resident permit costs 55 euros. Changes of address must be reported.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Netherby Council help desk.
