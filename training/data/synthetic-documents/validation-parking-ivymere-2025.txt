Ivymere Council - Residents' Parking Permits (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Ivymere Council

Ivymere Council serves the local area. Changes of address must be reported.

2. The number of permits per household

A household may hold at most 2 permits. Applications are processed within 8 working days. Enforcement officers patrol every day.

3. The price of a permit for a second car

A permit for a second car costs 200 euros. An annual resident permit costs 57 euros. Permits must be displayed in the windscreen.

4. How many visitor permits a household gets

Each household may receive 61 visitor permits per year. Changes of address must be reported.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Ivymere Council help desk.
