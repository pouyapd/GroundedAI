Fenwick Public Library - Quick Guide for New Members

Welcome to the library! Here are the basics.

Opening hours: Monday to Friday 09:00 to 19:00, Saturday 10:00 to 16:00. The library is closed on Sundays.

Renewals: you can renew an item twice, as long as nobody has reserved it.

Printing costs 10p per black-and-white page and 50p per colour page.

The study room on the first floor can be booked for up to three hours a day.
