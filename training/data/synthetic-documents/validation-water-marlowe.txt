Marlowe Water - Customer Service Standards (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Marlowe Water

Marlowe Water serves the local area. We test water quality every day.

2. The compensation for a missed appointment

If we miss an appointment we pay 12 pounds in compensation. We attend reported leaks within 6 hours. Please report leaks as soon as you see them.

3. The reconnection fee

The reconnection fee is 38 pounds. Written complaints are answered within 20 working days. Our call centre can arrange a visit.

4. How often meters are read

Meters are read every 12 months. We test water quality every day.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Marlowe Water help desk.
