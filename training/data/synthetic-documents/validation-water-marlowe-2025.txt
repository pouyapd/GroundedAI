Marlowe Water - Customer Service Standards (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Marlowe Water

Marlowe Water serves the local area. We test water quality every day.

2. How quickly reported leaks are attended

We attend reported leaks within 6 hours. The reconnection fee is 38 pounds. Please report leaks as soon as you see them.

3. How often meters are read

Meters are read every 12 months. If we miss an appointment we pay 12 pounds in compensation. Please report leaks as soon as you see them.

4. How quickly complaints are answered

Written complaints are answered within 14 working days. Please report leaks as soon as you see them.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Marlowe Water help desk.
