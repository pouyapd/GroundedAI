Bramblecot Day Nursery – Parent Handbook

Bramblecot Day Nursery cares for children aged 6 months to 5 years. The nursery is registered for 62 places and is owned by Bramblecot Nurseries Ltd. The Nursery Manager is Hannah Ruiz-Petit and the Deputy Manager is Gwen Rossiter-Mbeki. One of them is always on site while the nursery is open.

Children are cared for in three rooms. The Seedlings room is for babies aged 6 months to 2 years, with one adult for every 3 children. The Saplings room is for children aged 2 to 3 years, with one adult for every 4 children. The Oaks room is for children aged 3 to 5 years, with one adult for every 8 children. Children usually move up to the next room in the month after their birthday, after a two-week transition.

The nursery is open Monday to Friday, 07:30–18:30, for 51 weeks of the year. It closes for one week between Christmas and New Year and on all public holidays. Children can attend for a full day (07:30–18:30), a morning session (07:30–13:00) or an afternoon session (13:00–18:30). The minimum booking is two sessions per week.

Every child has a key person, a named practitioner who looks after the child's care and learning and is the main contact for the family. Parents can see photos and notes in their child's online learning journal in the Bramblecot Journal app, which is updated at least once a week. Parent meetings with the key person take place twice a year, in March and October.

Before a child starts, we offer two free settling-in sessions of up to two hours each. These take place in the two weeks before the start date, and a parent or carer stays for at least the first session. When a place is accepted, parents pay a registration fee of £50, which is not refundable, and a deposit of £150. The deposit is returned with the final invoice when the child leaves.

Fees depend on the child's age and the type of session. They are set out in the yearly fee notice; for example, a full day for a child under 2 costs £68.50. Fees are invoiced monthly in advance and must be paid by the 5th of each month. A late payment fee of £25 is added if the fees have not been paid by the 15th of the month.

Children must be collected by 18:30. A late collection charge of £15 is made for each 15 minutes, or part of 15 minutes, after 18:30. If a child has not been collected by 19:00 and we have not been able to contact the parents or emergency contacts, the nursery follows its uncollected child procedure.

Children may only be collected by the adults named on the registration form, who must be aged 16 or over. If someone else needs to collect your child, please phone the nursery in advance and give that person your family password. Without the password, staff will not release the child.

Children who are unwell should not attend. A child with a temperature of 38°C or above will be sent home. After vomiting or diarrhoea, a child must stay away from nursery until 48 hours after the last episode. Parents should tell the nursery by 09:00 if their child will be absent through illness.

Staff can give prescribed medicine only if it is in its original packaging with the pharmacy label, and only after a parent has completed form MED-1. Paracetamol that has not been prescribed can be given only with written consent, after staff have phoned a parent, and on no more than 3 days in a row.

All meals and snacks are included in the fees. Breakfast is served until 08:30, lunch at 11:45 and tea at 16:00. The nursery is nut-free, and all allergies must be recorded on the registration form and confirmed by a doctor's letter. Parents provide nappies and wipes, while the nursery provides sun cream unless a child needs a special one.

Children go outside every day unless the weather is dangerous, so please send them with clothes for all weather and a full set of spare clothes, all labelled with the child's name. Babies sleep according to their routine at home. Older children have a quiet rest time from 12:30 to 14:00, but no child is woken or kept awake against the parents' wishes.

Outings to the park and the library take place with written consent from parents, which is given once on the registration form. On outings, there is one adult for every 2 children under 2 and one adult for every 4 children aged 2 and over.

The Deputy Manager, Gwen Rossiter-Mbeki, is the Designated Safeguarding Lead. Staff and visitors may not use mobile phones in any of the children's rooms, and photos of children are taken only on nursery tablets.

All accidents are recorded, and parents are asked to sign the accident form on the same day. For any bump to the head, staff phone a parent straight away.

Parents must give at least four weeks' written notice to leave the nursery or to reduce sessions. If less notice is given, four weeks' fees are charged in place of notice.

If you have a concern, please speak to your child's key person or the Nursery Manager. Written complaints receive a reply within 10 working days. If you are still unhappy, you can write to the director of Bramblecot Nurseries Ltd, Leon Castellano-Wirth.
