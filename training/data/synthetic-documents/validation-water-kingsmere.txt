Kingsmere Water - Customer Service Standards

1. About Kingsmere Water

Kingsmere Water serves the local area. Please report leaks as soon as you see them.

2. How quickly reported leaks are attended

We attend reported leaks within 7 hours. If we miss an appointment we pay 20 pounds in compensation. We test water quality every day.

3. How often meters are read

Meters are read every 3 months. The reconnection fee is 78 pounds. We test water quality every day.

4. How quickly complaints are answered

Written complaints are answered within 13 working days. Our call centre can arrange a visit.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Kingsmere Water help desk.
