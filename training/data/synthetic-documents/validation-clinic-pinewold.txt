Pinewold Health Centre - Patient Information

1. About Pinewold Health Centre

Pinewold Health Centre serves the local area. Interpreters can be arranged.

2. The time the phone line opens

The phone line opens at 07:30. Repeat prescriptions are ready within 3 working days. The waiting room has step-free access.

3. The cancellation notice for appointments

Please cancel at least 21 hours before your appointment. The waiting room has step-free access.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Pinewold Health Centre help desk.
