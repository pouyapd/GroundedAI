Brindlewick Library Makerspace - Equipment Policy

Opening hours

The makerspace is open from Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00 to 18:00. Equipment must be switched off and cleaned 15 minutes before closing time.

3D printers

3D printer sessions can be booked for a maximum of two hours per session and up to three sessions per week. PLA filament is provided free of charge up to 200 g per member per month; after that it costs €0.05 per gram. Users aged 12 to 15 may use the 3D printers only when an adult is present. Prints must not be left running when the user leaves the room.

Laser cutter

The laser cutter may only be used after completing a 90-minute induction course. The laser cutter certification is valid for 12 months and must then be renewed with a short refresher session. The minimum age for using the laser cutter is 16. PVC and polycarbonate must never be cut with the laser cutter, because they release toxic and corrosive fumes. Approved materials are plywood up to 6 mm, acrylic and cardboard.

Electronics bench

Soldering irons must be returned to their stands when not in use. The fume extractor must be switched on during soldering.

Booking and cancellations

Bookings are made at the library desk or online. Members who miss two bookings without cancelling cannot book equipment for the following two weeks.
