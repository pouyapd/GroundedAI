Bramblecot Day Nursery – Fees and Sessions Notice 2026–27

These fees apply from 1 September 2026. They replace the fees for 2025–26 and stay in place until at least 31 August 2027. We review fees once a year and give parents at least 8 weeks' notice of any increase.

For children under 2, a full day (07:30–18:30) costs £68.50, a morning session (07:30–13:00) costs £41.00 and an afternoon session (13:00–18:30) costs £37.00. For children aged 2 and over, a full day costs £64.00, a morning session costs £38.50 and an afternoon session costs £34.50. The lower rate starts from the first day of the month after a child's second birthday.

Monthly invoices are the same each month. They are calculated by multiplying the weekly fee by 51 and dividing by 12. For example, three full days per week for a child under 2 cost £205.50 per week, which gives a monthly invoice of £873.38.

The morning session includes breakfast and lunch, and the afternoon session includes tea. All sessions include snacks, milk and drinks. There is no reduction for children who bring their own food for medical or religious reasons.

Families with two or more children at the nursery at the same time receive a sibling discount of 10%. The discount is taken off the fees of the older child. It does not apply to funded hours or to extra sessions.

Children who are eligible for funded hours can use 15 or 30 funded hours per week for 38 weeks of the year. Parents can instead choose to spread their funded hours across 51 weeks, which gives about 11.2 hours per week for a 15-hour entitlement and about 22.4 hours per week for a 30-hour entitlement. Families using funded hours pay a charge of £7.00 for each funded day to cover meals and consumables. Funded hours cannot be used for the late collection charge.

Two optional activities are charged in addition to the session fees. The music class on Wednesday mornings costs £4.50 per session, and Forest School for children in the Oaks room costs £6.00 per session on Fridays. Parents can sign up or stop at the start of each half term. Nappies and wipes are not included in the fees and must be provided by parents.

Requests to add regular sessions take effect from the 1st of the following month, if there is space. Requests to reduce sessions need at least four weeks' written notice, as for leaving the nursery. The minimum booking remains two sessions per week.

Extra sessions can be booked at least 48 hours in advance if there is space in the room. They are charged at the normal session price plus 10%. Extra sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged in full.

Fees are not reduced for sickness, holidays or days when the nursery is closed for public holidays. As the one exception, children who attend all year can take two weeks of holiday each year at half fees, as long as parents give at least four weeks' written notice. Unused holiday weeks do not carry over to the next year.

The nursery will be closed from Thursday 24 December 2026 to Friday 1 January 2027 and will reopen on Monday 4 January 2027. No fees are charged for the closure week, because it is not one of the 51 weeks for which the nursery is open.

Fees are invoiced monthly in advance and must be paid by the 5th of each month. A late payment fee of £25 is added to fees not paid by the 15th. If fees for two months remain unpaid, the nursery will send a written warning, and the place may be withdrawn 14 days after the warning.

You can pay by bank transfer, by childcare voucher or through a tax-free childcare account. Please always use your child's account number, which starts with BC, as the payment reference. We do not accept cash or cheques.

A registration fee of £50 and a deposit of £150 are paid when a place is accepted. The deposit is returned with the final invoice. The late collection charge is £15 for each 15 minutes, or part of 15 minutes, after 18:30.

Questions about fees should be sent to the Nursery Administrator, Pavel Nwosu-Hart, who is in the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday from 09:00 to 15:00. Invoices are sent by email on the 25th of the month before they are due.
