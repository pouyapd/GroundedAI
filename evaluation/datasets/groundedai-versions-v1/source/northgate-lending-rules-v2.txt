NORTHGATE LIBRARY - LENDING RULES
Version 2.0, January 2020.

1 Membership
Membership of Northgate Library is free for everyone who lives, works or studies in the borough.
Members need a library card to borrow items. A lost card is replaced for GBP 2.

2 Borrowing
Members can borrow up to 10 items at a time.
The loan period for books is 21 days.
Members MAY renew a loan twice, unless another member has reserved the item.
Reserved items are held for 7 days at the collection desk.

3 Late returns
A late fee of 20p per day is charged for each overdue adult book.
Children's books have no late fees.
The late fee for one item never exceeds GBP 6.

4 Damaged and lost items
Borrowers MUST return damaged items to the desk and not to the drop box.
A lost item is charged at its replacement cost plus an administration fee of GBP 3.

5 Opening hours
The library opens from 09:00 to 19:00 on weekdays and from 10:00 to 16:00 on Saturdays.
It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.
