Lindenfield Council - Residents' Parking Permits

1. About Lindenfield Council

Lindenfield Council serves the local area. Changes of address must be reported.

2. How many visitor permits a household gets

Each household may receive 43 visitor permits per year. A permit for a second car costs 175 euros. Changes of address must be reported.

3. The price of an annual permit

An annual resident permit costs 121 euros. Enforcement officers patrol every day.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Lindenfield Council help desk.
