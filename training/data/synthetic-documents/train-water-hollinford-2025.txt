Hollinford Water - Customer Service Standards (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Hollinford Water

Hollinford Water serves the local area. Please report leaks as soon as you see them.

2. The compensation for a missed appointment

If we miss an appointment we pay 34 pounds in compensation. We attend reported leaks within 4 hours. We test water quality every day.

3. The reconnection fee

The reconnection fee is 55 pounds. Written complaints are answered within 12 working days. We test water quality every day.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Hollinford Water help desk.
