Brindlewick Water District - Customer Questions and Answers

Billing and payments

How often will I receive a bill?
Customers are billed every two months. Each bill shows the meter readings, the water used in cubic metres and the fixed service charge.

Can I pay in instalments?
Yes. Customers who have difficulty paying can ask for a payment plan with up to six monthly instalments. There is no fee for a payment plan.

My bill is much higher than usual. Can I get a reduction because of a leak?
If a hidden leak on your property caused unusually high consumption, you can apply for a leak adjustment once every 12 months. The leak must be repaired within 30 days, and you need to send a plumber's invoice. The adjustment credits 50 percent of the excess usage compared with your average consumption.

Water quality

Is the tap water safe to drink?
The water is tested daily and meets all national drinking water standards. The water in Brindlewick is hard, with an average hardness of 180 mg/L of calcium carbonate. Hard water is safe to drink but can leave limescale in kettles.

What should I do during a boil water notice?
During a boil water notice, bring tap water to a rolling boil for 1 minute before drinking it or using it for cooking. Let the water cool before use. Boiled water can be stored in a clean, covered container for 24 hours. Water used for showering or washing clothes does not need to be boiled.

Connections and meters

How much does a new connection cost?
The standard fee for a new household connection is €450. The fee includes the meter and the first inspection. Work on private land is the responsibility of the owner.

Who reads my meter?
Meters are read remotely. If remote reading fails, a meter reader visits the property and shows an official district identity card.

Service interruptions

Planned work is announced on the district website at least 48 hours in advance. During interruptions, water tankers are placed at the Market Square and the Old Mill car park.
