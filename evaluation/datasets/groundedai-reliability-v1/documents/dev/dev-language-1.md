Dunmarra Language Centre – Student Handbook

Welcome to Dunmarra Language Centre. The Centre teaches English, Spanish, German and Japanese to adults and to young people aged 16 and over. Teaching is organised by the Director of Studies, Ines Moreau-Batista, and all questions about enrolment, fees, accommodation and certificates are handled by the Student Services Manager, Kofi Adjei-Brandt. The office is open Monday to Friday, 08:30–17:30.

Before your course starts you must take the online placement test. The test takes about 45 minutes and must be completed at least three days before your first lesson. If you have not taken it by then, you will take a paper test at 08:30 on your first Monday and start lessons one day later. There are six levels, from Level 1 (beginner) to Level 6 (advanced).

The Standard course has 15 hours of lessons per week. Classes run Monday to Friday from 09:15 to 12:30, with a break from 10:45 to 11:00. The Intensive course has 25 hours per week and adds afternoon workshops from 13:30 to 15:30, Monday to Friday. The Evening course has 4 hours per week, on Tuesday and Thursday from 18:30 to 20:30, and is sold in blocks of four weeks. New Standard and Intensive students can start on any Monday, while Evening blocks start on fixed dates.

Standard and Evening classes have a maximum of 12 students, and the afternoon workshops of the Intensive course have a maximum of 8 students. A class needs at least 4 students to run. If fewer than 4 students are booked for a level, the class runs as a mini-group with two thirds of the normal lesson hours, and the price does not change.

The Standard course costs €265 per week, the Intensive course costs €395 per week, and the Evening course costs €180 per four-week block. Every new student pays a registration fee of €60 once, and course books cost €38 per level. Students who book 12 weeks or more receive a 10% discount on tuition, and students who book 24 weeks or more receive a 15% discount. The discounts do not apply to Evening courses, the registration fee, books or accommodation.

The full course fee must be paid at least 14 days before the course starts. Students on courses of 24 weeks or more may pay in instalments instead, as explained in the fees guide. Payments by bank transfer must show your student number with the prefix DLC, for example DLC-40217, so that the payment can be matched to your account. Students who book late, less than 14 days before the start, must pay in full at the time of booking.

Regular attendance is required. You must attend at least 80% of your lessons to receive a course certificate. Students who hold a student visa must attend at least 85% of their lessons, and the Centre must report lower attendance to the immigration authorities. If you will be absent, please tell the office by 09:00 on the day. Students who arrive more than 15 minutes late are marked absent for the first lesson of the day.

The Centre is closed on public holidays, and lessons that fall on a public holiday are not made up or refunded. The Centre is also closed for the winter break from 21 December 2026 to 3 January 2027. Weeks during the winter break are not counted as part of your course, so the end date of a course that includes the break moves back by two weeks.

You can change from one course type to another with one week's notice, as long as there is space. Changes of level after your first week need the agreement of your teacher and the Director of Studies. Certificates are given out in class on the last Friday of your course, and a replacement certificate costs €15.

The Centre is an official test venue for the Dunmarra Proficiency Test (DPT). Registration for each DPT session closes six weeks before the exam date, and the exam fee is €145. Students who want to take the DPT should speak to their teacher about the right level before registering.

The Centre can arrange a room with a host family. Host family accommodation costs €210 per week for a single room with breakfast and evening meal, plus a one-off placement fee of €45. Requests must reach the office at least four weeks before arrival. All host families are within 40 minutes of the Centre by public transport.

Students aged 16 or 17 can join Standard and Intensive courses only. They must bring the parental consent form U18, signed by a parent or guardian, on their first day, and they must stay with a host family arranged by the Centre. Students under 18 may not leave their host family after 22:00 unless an adult from the family is with them.

The study centre on the second floor is open 08:00–19:00 from Monday to Friday and can be used by all students free of charge. A free conversation club takes place every Wednesday from 15:45 to 17:00. The social programme includes a guided excursion every Saturday, which costs between €25 and €40 depending on the destination and must be booked at reception by 12:00 on the Thursday before.

Students who hold a student visa must have health insurance for the full length of their course. The Centre can arrange insurance for €12 per week. Students must show proof of their own insurance, or buy the Centre's insurance, before their first lesson.

If you are unhappy with any part of your course, please speak first to your teacher. If the problem is not solved, write to the Student Services Manager at studentservices@dunmarra-lc.example. You will receive a written answer within 7 working days.
