Fernhollow Museum - Visitor Guide (Edition 2025)

This edition was published in 2025.

1. About Fernhollow Museum

Fernhollow Museum serves the local area. Photography without flash is allowed.

2. The price of an adult ticket

An adult ticket costs 12 euros. Children under 12 enter free. The shop sells books and prints.

3. The time of last entry

Last entry is at 15:00. The shop sells books and prints.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Fernhollow Museum help desk.
