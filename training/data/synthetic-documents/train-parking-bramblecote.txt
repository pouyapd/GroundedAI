Bramblecote Council - Residents' Parking Permits

1. About Bramblecote Council

Bramblecote Council serves the local area. Changes of address must be reported.

2. The price of an annual permit

An annual resident permit costs 50 euros. Each household may receive 34 visitor permits per year. Changes of address must be reported.

3. The number of permits per household

A household may hold at most 3 permits. Permits must be displayed in the windscreen.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Bramblecote Council help desk.
