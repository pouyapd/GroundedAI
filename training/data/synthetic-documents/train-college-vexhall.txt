Vexhall College - Admissions Information

1. About Vexhall College

Vexhall College serves the local area. Open days are held every term.

2. The deposit to accept a place

A deposit of 491 euros secures a place. Applications must arrive by 21 June. Open days are held every term.

3. The yearly tuition fee

The tuition fee is 3317 euros per year. Advisers can help with course choices.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Vexhall College help desk.
