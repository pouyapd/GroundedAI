Copperfield Makerspace - Membership Handbook

Opening hours
The makerspace is open from 10:00 to 22:00 on weekdays and from 09:00 to 18:00 at weekends. It is closed on public holidays.

Membership
Standard membership costs £35 per month and gives access during all opening hours. Off-peak membership costs £20 per month and is limited to weekdays before 16:00. Members can bring one guest per visit; guests must sign the visitor book and stay with the member at all times.

Equipment
Members may use the 3D printers, the electronics bench and the sewing machines without booking. The laser cutter and the CNC router must be booked through the members' portal, for at most two hours per session. Filament for the 3D printers is sold at reception at 5p per gram; members may also bring their own filament.

Inductions
Before using the laser cutter, every member must complete a one-hour laser induction, which costs £15. Members do not need an induction for the 3D printers.

Storage
Each standard member gets one storage box. Projects left on the shared tables overnight are moved to the lost property shelf and recycled after 30 days.

Leaving
Membership can be cancelled with one month's notice. The joining fee of £10 is not refunded.
