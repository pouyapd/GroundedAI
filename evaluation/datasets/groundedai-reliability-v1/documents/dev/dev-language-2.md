Dunmarra Language Centre – Fees, Payments and Cancellations: Questions and Answers

What do I pay when I book? You pay a registration fee of €60, the tuition for your course, and €38 for each course book you need. The registration fee is paid once, even if you book further courses later, and it is never refunded. If you book accommodation, you also pay the host family placement fee of €45.

How much is tuition? The Standard course (15 hours per week) costs €265 per week and the Intensive course (25 hours per week) costs €395 per week. Evening courses cost €180 per four-week block. If you book 12 weeks or more of Standard or Intensive lessons in one booking, you receive 10% off tuition, and for 24 weeks or more you receive 15% off. Two shorter bookings cannot be added together to reach a discount level.

When do I have to pay? Full payment is due at least 14 days before your course starts. For courses of 24 weeks or more, you can choose to pay in three instalments instead: 40% when you book, 30% before the start of week 8 and 30% before the start of week 16. If an instalment is more than 7 days late, your place may be suspended until it is paid.

How can I pay? You can pay by bank transfer or by card. Card payments of more than €1,000 carry a surcharge of 1.5%. Please always include your student number with the prefix DLC as the payment reference. We do not accept cash at the office.

What is included in the tuition fee? Tuition includes the placement test, all lessons, the course certificate, use of the study centre and Wi-Fi in the building. Course books, exam fees, excursions and accommodation are not included.

Can I change my course after I have booked? Before your course starts, you can change the course type or the start date free of charge. After the start, you can change course type with one week's notice if there is space. If the new course costs more, you pay the difference; if it costs less, the difference is given as a credit note. The first change after the start is free, and each further change costs €20.

What if I cancel before my course starts? If you cancel 28 days or more before the start date, we refund all tuition fees. If you cancel between 7 and 27 days before the start date, we refund 50% of tuition fees. If you cancel less than 7 days before the start date, no tuition is refunded. In every case the registration fee of €60 is kept, and books that have already been given to you are not refunded.

What if my visa is refused? If your student visa application is refused, we refund all fees you have paid except an administration charge of €150. To receive this refund, you must send us a copy of the refusal letter within 10 days of the date of the decision. This rule applies even if the course starts within 7 days.

Can I get money back if I leave my course early? We do not give cash refunds once a course has started. Instead, if you leave early, you receive a credit note for the unused full weeks of tuition. The credit note can be used for any course at the Centre and is valid for 12 months from the date it is issued.

Can I take a holiday during my course? Students on courses of 12 weeks or more can take one week of holiday for every 12 weeks booked. You must tell the office at least two weeks before the holiday. Your course end date moves back by the number of holiday weeks, and you do not pay extra for these weeks.

How much does the Dunmarra Proficiency Test cost? The DPT costs €145. The next two sessions are on Saturday 21 November 2026 and Saturday 6 March 2027, and registration closes six weeks before each date. Exam fees are only refunded if you are ill on the exam day and send a medical certificate within 5 working days, and the refund is 75% of the fee.

What does host family accommodation cost? A single room with breakfast and evening meal costs €210 per week, plus the €45 placement fee. If you cancel your host family less than 7 days before you arrive, you pay for one week. Extra nights before or after your booking cost €34 per night, if the family can offer them.

Do you offer airport transfers? Yes. A transfer from Dunmarra Airport to your accommodation costs €70 one way, and it must be booked at least 5 days before you arrive. If your flight is delayed, the driver waits for up to 90 minutes at no extra cost.

What if I lose my student card? A replacement student card costs €10 and is ready the next working day. You need your student card to enter the building after 17:30.
