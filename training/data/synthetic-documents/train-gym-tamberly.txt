Tamberly Fitness - Membership Terms (Edition 2022)

This edition was published in 2022.

1. About Tamberly Fitness

Tamberly Fitness serves the local area. Please wipe equipment after use.

2. The weekday opening time

On weekdays the gym opens at 06:00. The monthly membership fee is 45 euros. Please wipe equipment after use.

3. The price of a guest pass

A guest pass costs 16 euros per visit. Classes can be booked up to 2 days in advance. Staff can show you how to use the machines.

4. The cancellation notice period

Memberships can be cancelled with 20 days notice. Please wipe equipment after use.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Tamberly Fitness help desk.
