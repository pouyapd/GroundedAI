CORVANE SYSTEMS - ACCESS CONTROL STANDARD CS-AC-01
Revision B. Approved March 2024.

1 Scope
This standard applies to every system operated by Corvane Systems. It defines two assurance levels: AL1 for internal tools and AL2 for systems that hold customer data. It replaces Revision A of November 2018.

2 Passwords
Passwords SHALL be at least 14 characters long.
Accounts SHALL be locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts.
Passwords SHALL NOT be stored in plain text.

3 Sessions
At AL1, the inactivity timeout SHALL be no more than 4 hours.
At AL2, reauthentication SHALL occur after 1 hour of inactivity.
At AL2, the overall session timeout SHALL be no more than 12 hours, after which reauthentication is required.

4 Administrators
Administrators SHALL NOT share accounts.
Administrator access SHALL be reviewed every 6 months.
