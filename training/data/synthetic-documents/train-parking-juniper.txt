Juniper Point Council - Residents' Parking Permits

1. About Juniper Point Council

Juniper Point Council serves the local area. Enforcement officers patrol every day.

2. The number of permits per household

A household may hold at most 4 permits. An annual resident permit costs 86 euros. Permits must be displayed in the windscreen.

3. How many visitor permits a household gets

Each household may receive 44 visitor permits per year. Changes of address must be reported.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Juniper Point Council help desk.
