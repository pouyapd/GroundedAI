Aldervale Systems Travel and Expense Policy
Revision 4, March 2025. Owner: Finance Operations.

1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains how employees of Aldervale Systems book business travel and claim travel expenses. It applies to all permanent employees who travel for client work, conferences or internal meetings. Travel must have a clear business purpose and must be booked through the company travel portal unless the portal cannot offer a suitable option.

2. Approval before travel

Every trip needs approval from the employee's line manager before any booking is made. Trips with an estimated total cost above €2,000 also need pre-approval from Finance Operations. Requests for pre-approval should be sent at least ten working days before departure so that cheaper fares are still available.

3. Choosing the mode of transport

Rail is the preferred mode of transport for journeys of up to four hours. For longer journeys employees may fly. Economy class is the standard for all flights shorter than six hours. Business class may be booked for flights longer than six hours, but only with written approval from a director. Employees who use their private car for business trips are reimbursed at €0.30 per kilometre; parking and tolls are reimbursed separately against receipts.

4. Accommodation

Hotels should be booked through the travel portal. The hotel limit is €140 per night in capital cities and €110 per night in all other locations, including breakfast and local taxes. If an event makes hotels unusually expensive, the employee may exceed the limit by up to 20 percent with line-manager approval. Employees staying with friends or family instead of a hotel cannot claim an accommodation allowance.

5. Meals and daily allowance

Employees receive a daily meal allowance of €45 for each full day of travel. For travel days shorter than eight hours the allowance is €30. If a meal is provided free of charge, for example at a conference, the allowance is reduced by €15 for each provided lunch or dinner. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

6. Receipts and claims

Receipts are required for any single expense above €25. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days after the end of the trip using the LedgerLeaf expense tool. Claims submitted later than 30 days need a written explanation and may be rejected. Foreign currency expenses are converted at the rate shown on the card statement.

7. Insurance and safety

All business trips are covered by the company travel insurance provided by Halden Assist. Employees must register trips abroad in the travel portal before departure so that the insurer can contact them in an emergency. For destinations with an official travel warning, travel needs approval from the Chief Operating Officer.

8. Policy questions

Questions about this policy should be sent to Finance Operations through the internal service desk.
