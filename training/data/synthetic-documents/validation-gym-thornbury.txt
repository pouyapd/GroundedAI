Thornbury Fitness - Membership Terms

1. About Thornbury Fitness

Thornbury Fitness serves the local area. Lockers are emptied every night.

2. The cancellation notice period

Memberships can be cancelled with 23 days notice. New members pay a one-time joining fee of 34 euros. Lockers are emptied every night.

3. How far ahead classes can be booked

Classes can be booked up to 5 days in advance. A guest pass costs 10 euros per visit. Please wipe equipment after use.

4. The weekday opening time

On weekdays the gym opens at 06:00. The monthly membership fee is 47 euros. Staff can show you how to use the machines.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Thornbury Fitness help desk.
