## Limitations

- Synthetic cases written by a separate agent of the same model family as the checks' author; shared blind spots are possible. A blind audit of 50 test labels estimates label quality.
- Subtypes were chosen around known failure modes, so the mix does not reflect how often each case occurs in real use; overall rates are not real-world rates.
- The checks are lexical and pattern-based. A good score does not show that they understand meaning, and results on this suite do not prove generalisation to other documents.
- Component-level only: fixed passages and fixed answers; no language model was run.
