Upton Vale Fitness - Membership Terms

1. About Upton Vale Fitness

Upton Vale Fitness serves the local area. Staff can show you how to use the machines.

2. How far ahead classes can be booked

Classes can be booked up to 11 days in advance. The monthly membership fee is 30 euros. Lockers are emptied every night.

3. The cancellation notice period

Memberships can be cancelled with 30 days notice. Lockers are emptied every night.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the Upton Vale Fitness help desk.
